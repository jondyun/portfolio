Projects

One Click Printer

Custom built 3D printer with a webapp that allows 3D files to be sliced and printed through one button. The system is designed for accessibility, with the printer being minimal and extremely low-cost, and the webapp condensing the 3D printing workflow into an intuitive process.

FDM Ducted Fan with 1 kg Thrust

With a self-designed thrust testing lever, I built a ducted fan with a simple outrunner motor and a cheap 3D printer. My printing processes and inlet, exhaust, cone, and stator design have been slowly iterated upon for years now, and I was able to use the lip for my final CFD project in undergrad!

Arduino Thrust-Vectoring Monocopter

Contra-rotating Ducted Fan

After running into adverse torque issues with my monocopter, I set out to build a contra-rotating fan that would increase thrust and mitigate torque on the fan axis. I used a bevel gear setup with SLS printed parts and conducted at-home thrust testing.
